# Stock take corrects one Location at a time, with no cross-location move primitive

When a pantry item's quantity has drifted from reality, the visible symptom is often "this got moved from the garage to the shelf and nobody logged it" — which suggested modeling Stock take as a location-to-location transfer. Instead, Stock take only ever sets a single Location's row to an absolute observed count and never touches that item's rows at other Locations: reconciling each Location independently already keeps the item's total (Quantity, a derived sum per ADR 0005) accurate once every affected Location has been stock-taken, with no transfer entity or history log needed. A dedicated move-between-locations primitive isn't ruled out for the future, but nothing in Stock take requires one.
